# Webshite
I'm tired of my isolation

zo3bubbl3's corner of the internet: streams, videos, games and projects.

## Editing the site
Everything you'd want to change is at the top of the `<script>` in `index.html`, under **EDIT YOUR STUFF HERE**:
`GOALS`, `SOCIALS`, `PROJECTS`, `POSTCARDS` and `PINS`.

## Pictures
- `postcards/` – postcard fronts (any shape, the card matches the picture)
- `pins/` – pin photos (cut-out PNGs look best on the board)
- `media/` – screenshots and clips for project pages
