Puts you and your callers on an old telephone line. Load callers' voice clips on the switchboard, ring them in, and answer.

Audio

For OBS, send this to a device you don't listen to, or to a virtual cable like "CABLE Input". OBS's Application Audio Capture still picks it up either way.

Hand signals

Hold a sign for400 ms

With the camera on, the voice filter stays off until you pick up. Touch your cheek (or flash a peace sign) to ring the next caller. Make the phone hand, thumb and pinky out, to pick up and get on the line. Make a finger gun, thumb and index out, to put the caller through and start their clip. Make the phone hand again to hang up. Each sign has to be held briefly and let go before it can fire again.

Record

Saves a .webm of what this page sends out: your line, the ring, callers and dial tone. With the camera on it adds your camera picture, clean, with none of the hand-tracking marks. With the camera off, or Camera in recording off, it saves sound only. The file downloads when you press Stop.

Room light

Idle
Brightness100%

Opens a plain window of solid colour. Drag it onto your second monitor and double-click it to go full screen (Esc to leave). It follows the call: it swells with each ring and changes colour when you pick up and when the caller talks. Blow a kiss at the camera (fingertips to your lips, then away) to switch to dark red. Blow another to switch back. A ring still shows through the kiss mood.

Ghost-show overlays

Overlay link: alert server not running

When the overlay set's alert server is running, this page tells the ghost-show overlays in OBS what the phone is doing. The caller lower-third shows who's ringing and on the line, the frames glow when it rings, the EMF meter jumps, and kiss mood turns the fog red. It connects by itself.

Haunted ring

Haunt
Ring volume

Picking up (P or the big button) answers and cuts the ring off instantly. Higher Haunt makes the bells detune, sag, stutter and echo.

Calls

Dial tone length

With Pick up first on, answering stops the ring and puts you on the line while the caller waits. Press P again to put them through. Dial tone plays down your line after a caller hangs up or you hang up on them.
