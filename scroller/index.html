How to use Scroller

The feed

  • ScrollSwipe↑ ↓ next and previous
  • ClickTapSpace pause and play
  • ← → skip 5 seconds back or forward
  • M sound on or off. It starts muted so videos can autoplay.
  • F fill the screen, or show the whole frame
  • A auto: move on when a video ends. Off, each one loops.
  • L♥ like
  • 1–5 star rating. Hover the star to pick one with the mouse.
  • R top rated first S shuffle
  • Click the thin bar at the bottom to jump to that point.

Live TV

  • Every subfolder is a channel, plus one called Everything.
  • Channels play on the clock, like real TV. Tune in and you land part-way through whatever is on.
  • ↑ ↓Swipe change channel. Tapping the top or bottom of the screen does too.
  • 1–9 jump to a channel
  • G the guide: what's on now and next on every channel
  • M sound F fill

Good to know

  • Likes, stars and your settings are kept in this browser.
  • Some phone videos (HEVC .mov) won't play in every browser. The card says so instead of staying black.
  • Chrome and Edge can reopen your last folder in one click.
  • Esc goes back to the start. T switches between the feed and Live TV.
