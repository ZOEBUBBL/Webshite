SCROLLER
Your own videos and photos, one swipe at a time. Or tune in to them like TV.


START
  1. Double-click "Open Scroller.html". It opens in your web browser.
  2. Click "Try the samples" to see how it works, or "Choose a folder" to watch your own.
  That's it. There is nothing to install.

  Works best in Chrome or Edge (they can reopen your last folder in one click).
  Firefox and Safari work too.


YOUR FILES STAY YOURS
  Scroller is a single web page that runs on your own computer. Your videos and photos are read straight from your
  disk by your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. Likes, stars and settings are kept in your browser.


THE FEED
  One video or photo fills the screen. Scroll, swipe or press the arrow keys for the next one.
  Click or tap to pause. Sound starts off so videos can play by themselves: press M or the speaker button.
  Heart (L) to like, stars (1 to 5) to rate. "Top rated" and "Shuffle" are at the top.
  Auto (A) moves on by itself when a video ends; photos stay up for a few seconds.

LIVE TV
  Every subfolder of the folder you chose becomes a channel, plus one called Everything.
  Channels run on the clock like real TV: tune in and you land part-way through whatever is on.
  Up and down (or swipe) to change channel. G shows the guide: what's on now and next everywhere.

  Tip: make subfolders like "Holidays", "Funny", "Music videos" and each becomes its own channel.

Press ? at any time for every control.


WHAT PLAYS
  Video: mp4 (best), webm, m4v, mov, ogv.  Photos: jpg, png, gif, webp, avif.
  Some phone videos (HEVC .mov) won't play in every browser. Scroller tells you instead of showing a black screen.


ON A PHONE
  Put the Scroller folder online (any website host works, the files are all it needs) and open it on your phone,
  then use "Choose files" to pick videos from your camera roll.
